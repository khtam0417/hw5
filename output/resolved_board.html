What "resolved" means here

This is a draft-only simulation. Sending vendor or customer messages is outside its scope and is never required to complete the shop's internal work. A ticket is resolved for internal processing when the Boss, through the agent and MCP workflow, has confirmed four things: every payment the ticket needs is paid, every vendor draft is complete, the latest customer draft is accurate, and no decision is open. No status was set by hand. Resolution never means that anything was sent, ordered, shipped, delivered or sold, or that a customer accepted anything.

  • 101: invoice 501 is paid, the reprint request (draft #3) is complete, and the replacement customer update (draft #4) is accurate. The Boss checked all three in run 4 and resolved the ticket. The drafts remain unsent, and no order, shipment or delivery is confirmed.
  • 102: the rent was paid once, after a person's approval. That is everything the shop owed.
  • 103: the owner declined the 20 that can't be supplied, the Boss recorded it, and no decision is left open. The 10% offer for 8 stays an unsent alternative, not a sale.

Before the owner's clarification, the Boss applied the earlier criteria in run 3 and kept 101 open, because it counted sending the vendor order as shop-side work. That screenshot is kept below.
